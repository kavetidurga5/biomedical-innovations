Prior work

DDH Task Trainer

Our first project: a low-cost, 3D-printed simulation trainer for Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip, designed to give learners tactile practice on the Ortolani and Barlow maneuvers before encountering a real patient.

3D CAD Model

UTSW Ginsburg Forum — April 2026

Physical Prototype

Clinical Problem

DDH is treatable with a Pavlik harness when caught early — but delayed diagnosis leads to surgery. The key exam findings are subtle and require deliberate practice most trainees never get.

The Gap

Most trainees encounter DDH infrequently during medical school. No widely available, affordable simulation tool existed for practicing the physical exam before clinical rotations.

Our Approach

A 3D-printed task trainer with haptic feedback for both Ortolani and Barlow findings — built in collaboration with Dr. Kim Ngoc Van Horn, EM faculty at Children's Health.

Status

Prototype complete. Presented at the UTSW Ginsburg Forum (April 2026). Continuing refinement in collaboration with Dr. Van Horn ahead of conference presentations.
