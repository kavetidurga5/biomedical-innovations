Prior work

DDH Task Trainer

Our first project: a low-cost, 3D-printed simulation trainer for Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip, designed to give learners tactile practice on key diagnostic maneuvers before encountering a real patient.

Physical Prototype

3D CAD Model

Clinical Problem

DDH is treatable with a Pavlik harness when caught early — but delayed diagnosis leads to surgery. The Barlow and Ortolani maneuvers are the key physical exam findings: Barlow dislocates an unstable hip posteriorly, while Ortolani reduces a dislocated hip with a palpable clunk. These findings require deliberate practice most trainees never get.

The Gap

Most trainees encounter DDH infrequently during medical school. No widely available, affordable simulation tool existed for practicing these maneuvers before clinical rotations.

Our Approach

A 3D-printed task trainer with haptic feedback for both positive and negative Barlow and Ortolani findings — built in collaboration with Dr. Kim Ngoc Van Horn, Pediatric Emergency Medicine faculty at Children's Health.

Status

Prototype complete. Presented at UTSW Ginsburg Forum (April 2026). Accepted to present at UTMB Simulation Summit (July 2026) and IMSH 2027.
